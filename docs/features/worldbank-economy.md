The World Bank
 The World Bank is a feature added in the 1.2.6 update of GeneratorCraft. 
- Every world has a default world bank amount of 1,000,000
- The World Bank only deals in Coins

The state of the World Bank's currency affect the Economy Boost and the Economy State:
- <-500,000 = Economy Boost: 0.1x, Economy State: Collapsed
- <0 = Economy Boost: 0.25x, Economy State: Debt Crisis
- <200,000 = Economy Boost: 0.5x, Economy State: Depression
- <500,000 = Economy Boost: 0.75x, Economy State: Recession
- <1,000,000 = Economy Boost: 1x, Economy State: Stable
- <2,000,000 = Economy Boost: 1.25x, Economy State: Booming
- <5,000,000 = Economy Boost: 1.5x, Economy State: Overheated
- <10,000,000 = Economy Boost: 2x, Economy State: Inflated
- >9,999,999 = Economy Boost: 3x, Economy State: Hyperinflation

If the Economy Boost stat is above 1x, it will only affect the prices of things (ex. upgrades, shop items)

If the Economy Boost is below 1x, it will only affect the output of things (ex. coins from mob kill, daily rewards) The World Bank may be affected with the /loan command ( 
#loan
 ) or by adding or removing coins via purchasing upgrades, shop items, killing mobs or obtaining rewards Reaching your coin limit in the bank or in your wallet will give the extra coins to the bank
