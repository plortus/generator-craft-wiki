Rebirth
 Rebirth is a feature added in the 1.2.5 update of GeneratorCraft. 
- Upon reaching GeneratorCraft Level (10*(Rebirths+1)) you unlock the option to Rebirth
- Rebirthing will reset your stats, advancements, recipes, shop unlocks and GeneratorCraft Level back to their default, but increase your Rebirth Boost by 0.1
- Rebirth Boost is a multiplier that gets added to Coin and Gem drop from mobs and farming
