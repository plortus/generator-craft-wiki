GeneratorCraft Level
 Or in short: GC Level. Mostly cosmetic features that shows your progression in GeneratorCraft. You get GeneratorCraft XP for doing pretty much anything in the mod. You get 1 XP for specific actions. Once you reach 5 XP you gain a Level. Your Level color changes the more you level up (See 
#levelcolors
