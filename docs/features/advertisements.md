Advertisements
 Advertisements are an annoying popup that will appear until the Advertisement Blocker has been purchased from the GeneratorCraft Store ( 
#generatorcraft-store
 ). While viewing an ad, the player may not take damage. The GUI cannot be closed in any way unless the skip button has been pressed After skipping an ad, a small reward of (a random number between 1 to 25) coins and gems is received
