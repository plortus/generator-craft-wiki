## Changes:

- Gem and Fortunite armor are now trimmable
- Added a new mob variant: Wealthy. Zombies, Skeletons, and Creepers may spawn with a fancy suit. They are slightly tougher than their regular counterparts but provide more loot upon death
- Gem Core Shrine can now only generate in Desert or Biome of Wealth
- The Hoarder's GUI now displays the item they are looking to buy
- Added the Gemstone Obelisk: a new trophy Gem Shop item for the low price of 100,000 Gems

- You may convert it back to 100,000 Gems using /convertobelisk

- Right-Clicking the Obelisk sets an owner to it, ensuring you can display it at full glory without worrying about thieves
- Added the FrikandelBroodje Speciaal sword (Weapons Lootbox)
- Added the Sword and Crown of Greed (Gilded Crown/Sword of Sinful Greediness). They may drop from Wealthy mobs
- Added a new enchantment: RNG Miner

- It goes up to level 3, conflicts with Silk Touch and Fortune

- It can be obtained through the Enchantment Table, Existing book loot tables, and villager trades

- When mining ores, there's a (1/(50,000-(EnchantLevel*10,000))) chance for an RNG rain to trigger
