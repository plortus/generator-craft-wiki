Changes: 
- Gem Shop is temporarily disabled
- Added a new mob: the Money Sheep

- The Money Sheep is a Green and Gold wild sheep, cursed a long time ago by Greed

- It may spawn randomly in your world (more common in the Biome of Wealth) unless the difficulty is Peaceful

- It will attack only when attacked and deal a lot of damage

- When killed has a low chance to drop 1-128 Money Pouches and/or Gems

- Stats: 50 Health, 25 Damage, 0.5 Armor Protection

- Cannot be bred or farmed in any way
- Added Gambling Dice:

- Roll a number between 1-6 for the small cost of 2000 coins (tier 1) or 800 gems (tier 2)

- Rolling any number that is not 6 will keep the dice, but give you nothing

- Rolling a 6 will give you a reward and use the dice
- Added a fun sound when you win in Slots
