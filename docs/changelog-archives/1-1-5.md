## Changes:

- Changed the texture of the Bankophone
- The Hoarder will no longer close its GUI every time you sell an item
- Added the Hoarder's Contact: can be bought in the Hoarder's GUI for 1,000 coins and is used to open the Hoarder's GUI quicker
- The Hoarder will no longer buy Wooden Pickaxes, Cakes, and Spyglasses. Those have been replaced by Wheat Seeds and Dirt
- Additionally, the Hoarder will now also buy Andesite, Diorite, and Granite
- The TechnoBlade, Might of a Thousand Worlds, and the Worn Out Axe have received placeholder ability item descriptions, until they get given abilities
- Added a new Generator: the Redstone Generator
- Generators now have Upgrades: Storage, Speed, and Compacting:

- Craft the new Generator Upgrade Unit and place it above an active generator to start upgrading it (you also get an advancement!)

- There are three types of upgrades: Compacting, Speed, and Storage, all with their own unique recipes

- The Compacting upgrade turns the output into its compacted form (if it has any)

- The Speed upgrades go from Stone -> Gold -> Diamond and increase the output amount of the generator

- The Storage upgrades go from Tier 1 -> Tier 9, with each tier adding an extra slot to a generator
- Buffed the Bank coin and gem limits by 10x
- You may now click on player messages' level number in chat to see the Level Colors menu

 

## Fixes:

- Fixed Ender Pearl Generator being impossible since pearls can only stack to 16
- Fixed not getting GeneratorCraft XP when upgrading the bank
