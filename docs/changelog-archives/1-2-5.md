## Changes:

- Abandoned Slot Machine finally has its own Loot Table
- Changed how the chat looks when killing mobs and currency chances proc
- Added a new feature: Rebirths:

- Upon reaching GeneratorCraft Level (10 × (Rebirths + 1)), you unlock the option to Rebirth

- Rebirthing will reset your stats, advancements, recipes, shop unlocks, and GeneratorCraft Level back to default

- But you'll gain a Rebirth Boost of +0.1

- Rebirth Boost is a multiplier added to Coin and Gem drops from mobs and farming

 

## Fixes:

- Money Logs and Leaves dropped Slot Tokens even when mined with Silk Touch
- Fixed mob killing rewards (Coin, Gem, Box Chance, etc.) and adjusted some values
