Changes: 
- Looting now affects coin and gem drops! The formula is CoinDrop+((1.5xLootingLevel)+Random(1-10), GemDrop+((1xLootingLevel)+Random(1-10)
- Added an Update Checker which should activate upon launching the game and will notify you if a newer version of GeneratorCraft is available
- Added a gamerule: coinMultiplierLimit. Default is 15
- Moved Lootbox drops from Procedure to Loottable. (Side Effects: will no longer display drop chance. Won't be annoying to change anymore)
- Added a funny Watermark overlay that will appear on your screen. You may visit to shop to remove it for the low price of 500 Coins and 20 Gems
