Gambling Dice
 Gambling Dice is an item that rolls a number between 1-6 for a price. It can be obtained from various loottables If the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 are rolled, nothing happens. If the numbers 6 is rolled, a reward is given. A tier 1 dice costs 2,000 coins to roll and rewards 25,000 coins A tier 2 dice costs 800 gems to rolls and rewards 2,000 gems
