Big Gem
 The Big Gem is a trophy item that can be bought only once per world from the Gem Shop ( 
#gem-shop
 ). Once bought, a serverwide message announces who bought it. It is a block, meaning you can display it as a trophy alongside a dragon egg or a beacon. It has gravity, and is about as strong as glass.
